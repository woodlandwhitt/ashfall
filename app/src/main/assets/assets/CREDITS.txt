Ashfall: Meridian — third-party assets. All are CC0 1.0 / public domain: no attribution required, credited anyway.

Textures (assets/tex): ambientCG.com — Ground054, Ground103, Rock058, Asphalt023S, Plaster007, Concrete047A, Wood094, Bricks097, Metal055A.
  Downsized to 1024px, ambient occlusion baked into colour, some colour-graded for the desert.
Gunshots (assets/sfx/*.mp3 named after guns): The Free Firearm Sound Library (opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library).
Footsteps (assets/sfx/step_*): Fantozzi's Footsteps (opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone).
Gun models (assets/guns): "Flat Guns" East and West CC0 packs on OpenGameArt (opengameart.org/content/cc0-flat-guns-west, /cc0-flat-guns-east).
  The above were collected via github.com/petroulacl/fps-asset-kit.
HD sand, dirt, rock, weathered planks and bark (assets/tex, assets/tex2k): ambientCG and Poly Haven CC0 materials, via github.com/RazorEdge-wyh/unity-nature-pbr-asset-library.
UI, door, lock, cloth, card, chip and dice sounds (assets/sfx/k_*): Kenney (kenney.nl) RPG, UI and Casino audio packs, via github.com/iwenzhou/kenney.
