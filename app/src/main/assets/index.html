A WASTELAND RPG

  

  
Left side of the screen moves, right side looks. Hold FIRE to shoot, AIM for accuracy, FOCUS to freeze time and pick your shots. Best played in landscape.

  
Keyboard: WASD move, mouse look, click fire, right-click aim, V focus, C sneak, G grenade, E use, R reload, Q swap, F heal, Tab pack, Esc pause, ` console, F2 developer menu.






  
The bombs fell on a Tuesday. Nobody alive remembers which one.

  
For two hundred years the four hundred people of Haven 12 waited behind a steel door, growing mushrooms and telling each other the surface was gone.

  
This morning the water recycler failed. Engineering says the coolant regulator is finished and there is no spare. The Overseer handed you a pistol, a jumpsuit and a wrist computer older than your grandmother, and the door opened for the first time.

  
Find a regulator. Find people. Try not to die.
