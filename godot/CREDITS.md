# Credits (Godot build)

All third-party assets are free and need no sign-up.

- **People and animations:** Quaternius, CC0 1.0. Universal Base Characters (Standard), Modular Character
  Outfits - Fantasy (Standard) and Universal Animation Library (Standard). Obtained via the GitHub mirrors
  Mesh2Motion/mesh2motion-assets, dtrajko/MoravaEngine, glanderness/BeefTV and dramaclaw/dramaclaw.
  See chars/LICENSE_Quaternius.txt. The extra outfits (Noble, Wizard, Knight cloth), hair styles and the
  second animation library (UAL2) are the same Quaternius CC0 packs, obtained via kirbycope/godot-npc-village.
- **Guns:** Quaternius "Ultimate Guns", CC0 (same models as the WebView build). Build 13's guns (guns/lp_*.glb)
  are the "low-poly guns" from zees-games/zee-dot-weapons, kitbashed from Quaternius's Ultimate Low Poly Guns
  Pack, CC0 (guns/LICENSE-zee-low-poly-guns.txt). The energy weapons and two grenades (guns/bl/) are Kenney's
  Blaster Kit, CC0; the melee tools and blades (guns/ml/) are Kenney's Survival Kit and Weapon Pack, CC0 (licence
  files alongside), both via the same repository. All are repainted in game.
- **Textures, props, sounds and world models:** exported from the WebView build. Their sources and licences are
  listed in assets/CREDITS.txt at the repository root (Poly Haven CC0, Kenney CC0, ambientCG CC0 and others).
- **Furniture and clutter (props/*.glb):** photoscanned models from Poly Haven (CC0), via
  github.com/RazorEdge-wyh/unity-nature-pbr-asset-library, converted and reduced for mobile. The wasteland kit
  (props/wasteland_kit.glb: barrels, crates, pallets, tyres, rubbish) is Quaternius and Kenney (CC0), via the WebView build.
- **The Silver Spur:** card, chip, dice and shuffle sounds (sfx/k_card*, k_chip*, k_dice, k_shuffle) are Kenney's
  Casino Audio (CC0); the lounge music (sfx/x_lounge*) is Kenney Music Loops (CC0); the room's murmur (sfx/o_crowd)
  is from the Oculus Audio Pack. All via the WebView build; see assets/CREDITS.txt.
- **Voices:** generated offline with Piper (github.com/rhasspy/piper, MIT licence) using its "en-us-libritts-high"
  voice from the Piper GitHub release v0.0.2. That voice was trained on LibriTTS (Zen et al., 2019,
  openslr.org/60), CC BY 4.0. Each character speaks with one of its 904 speakers. See tools/voice/.
- **Radio music** (music/), from filmmusic.io under its standard licence (CC BY 4.0: free with attribution), obtained
  via SomeTroglodyte/Additional-Music-Various on GitHub:
  - "Apocalypse Blues", "Sunset Fields", "The Northern Path" by Alexander Nakarada (filmmusic.io)
  - "Horsemen Approach" and "Tavern Brawl" by Alexander Nakarada feat. Kevin MacLeod (filmmusic.io)
  - "Teller of the Tales", "Devonshire Waltz Allegretto", "Grand Dark Waltz Allegro", "Adeste Fideles Waltz",
    "Suonatore di Liuto" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com / filmmusic.io)
  - "Waltz Primordial" by Kevin MacLeod feat. Alexander Nakarada (filmmusic.io)
  Licence: https://filmmusic.io/standard-license
- **Engine:** Godot Engine 4.5, MIT licence.
